Community library series

Microlibraries

SHAU / Daliana Suryawinata and Florian Heinzelmann
Indonesia

The series pairs accessible reading rooms with other community uses. At Warak Kayu, the elevated library leaves a sheltered ground level for workshops, presentations and film screenings, allowing a small footprint to accommodate different activities.

The programme treats each library as a local community building rather than a standard repeated object. At Bima, reused ice-cream buckets form the facade; at Warak Kayu, a raised timber structure separates reading above from shared activity below while maintaining visual connections.
