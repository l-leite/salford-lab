Culture and leisure centre · 1982–86

SESC Pompeia

Lina Bo Bardi
São Paulo, Brazil

A culture and leisure centre combining retained factory sheds with new sports facilities and spaces for communal use.

Lina Bo Bardi retained the factory sheds and added new sports buildings. The project brings new communal uses into an industrial setting while preserving its material character, making reuse part of its social and architectural position.
