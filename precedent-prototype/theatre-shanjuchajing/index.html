Tea shop and tasting rooms

théATRE SHANJUCHAJING Concept Store

kooo architects / Shinya Kojima and Ayaka Kojima
Beijing, China

Customers browse tea displays, learn about tea varieties and sit opposite servers at a low tasting bar. More private rooms provide a quieter setting for tea and conversation.

The walls use specially developed tea-earth bricks, combining tea leaves with compressed soil. Their changing colour and visible leaf particles connect the material interior to the product. Circulation moves from an open display area through a lounge to a concealed, daylit tea room.
