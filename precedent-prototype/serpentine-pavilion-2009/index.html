Temporary public pavilion · 2009

Serpentine Gallery Pavilion 2009

SANAA
London, UK

The pavilion provides a shared outdoor setting for gathering and public events. Access from every side and movable seating allow different arrangements beneath one continuous canopy, while views remain open across the park.

Mirror-finished aluminium reflects the trees, sky and people beneath the roof, making its appearance change with the surroundings. The apparently thin surface is a sandwich of aluminium and plywood, supported by slender columns and shaped around existing trees.
