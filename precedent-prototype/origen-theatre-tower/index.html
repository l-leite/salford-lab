Temporary mountain theatre · 2017

Origen Theatre Tower

Giovanni Netzer; structural design Walter Bieler; construction Uffer / QUADRIN
Julier Pass, Switzerland

The tower provides an enclosed venue for Origen's theatrical performances in a high mountain pass. Audiences gather around a vertically organised interior rather than a conventional horizontal auditorium.

The striking red tower was assembled from around 900 timber components at an altitude of 2,284 metres. Conceived as a temporary theatre, it treats the mountain pass and changing seasons as part of its cultural setting rather than a backdrop to be excluded.
