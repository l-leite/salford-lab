Museum and public cultural spaces · 2025

Abby Kortrijk

Barozzi Veiga and TAB Architects
Kortrijk, Belgium

Exhibitions occupy historic rooms and new underground galleries. A public living room, workshop spaces and café add everyday uses, allowing the museum to host artistic work, presentations and informal encounters as well as the display of art.

Much of the new exhibition accommodation is underground, allowing the historic chapel, dormitories and courtyard to remain legible above. A new brick pavilion echoes the surrounding pitched roofs while giving the café a distinct identity facing the park.
