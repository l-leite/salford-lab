Biennale exhibition pavilion · 1962

Nordic Pavilion

Sverre Fehn
Venice, Italy

The pavilion hosts art and architecture exhibitions within a largely open room. Its restrained interior and filtered daylight provide a distinctive setting for presenting work and moving between exhibits.

Two perpendicular layers of slender concrete beams filter daylight while spanning the exhibition room. Openings in the roof accommodate tree trunks. The structure therefore defines both the lighting conditions for artworks and the experience of the room, making roof and atmosphere inseparable.

Sverre Fehn — original project publication, Arkitektur N ↗
