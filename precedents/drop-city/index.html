Artists' community · 1965

Drop City

Drop City collective
Near Trinidad, Colorado, USA

Artists, writers and inventors established a settlement for communal living and creative work. Building experiments, artworks and shared activities developed together, making the community an ongoing practical investigation rather than a conventional collection of separate studios.

Residents adapted geodesic and crystalline geometries using salvaged timber, bottle caps and cut-out car roofs. Limited money and little building experience encouraged improvisation, connecting a rejection of consumer culture with experiments in lightweight enclosure and reused materials.
