Atmospheric research laboratory · 2005

Cloud Laboratory

Schulz und Schulz
Leipzig, Germany

Scientists investigate cloud formation and human influences on the atmosphere using a controlled simulator. Offices for analysis surround specialist laboratories where physical experiments take place.

The cylindrical tower houses a vertical flow tube that requires stable environmental conditions. Its height and windowless enclosure come directly from the experiment inside; the lower ring of laboratories and offices makes the scientific apparatus legible as the building's organising centre.
