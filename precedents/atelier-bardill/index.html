Musician's studio · 2007

Atelier Bardill

Valerio Olgiati
Scharans, Switzerland

The atelier provides one room for Linard Bardill's creative work beside an open courtyard. A modest amount of enclosed working space is paired with a much larger outdoor room for light, outlook and pause.

Planning permission required the replacement to keep the old barn's volume, although the client needed less than a third of it. Olgiati turned the remainder into a courtyard with a large circular opening to the sky, giving an apparently solid building an unexpectedly open centre.
