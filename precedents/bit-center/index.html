Offices and lecture space · 1998

BIT Center

Alberto Campo Baeza
Inca, Mallorca, Spain

Glazed offices provide places for concentrated work within a sheltered garden. A sunken amphitheatre in the courtyard introduces a collective setting for lectures and presentations alongside the everyday workspace.

A triangular stone enclosure contains an orthogonal grid of columns and lemon trees. The contrast between a heavy travertine podium and light roofs gives the project its order, while services run below the floor to keep the workspaces clear.
