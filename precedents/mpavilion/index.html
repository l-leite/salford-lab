Public events pavilion · 2017

MPavilion

OMA / Rem Koolhaas and David Gianotten
Melbourne, Australia

The pavilion accommodates performances, talks and public events at an intimate scale. Its movable seating changes the arrangement of gatherings and allows audience and stage to exchange positions.

One grandstand is embedded in a planted mound while the other rotates beneath the roof. Rather than treating flexibility as an empty floor, the design gives a specific architectural element movement, allowing the same small venue to produce contrasting social and performance arrangements.
