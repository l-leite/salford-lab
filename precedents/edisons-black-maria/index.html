Motion-picture studio · 1893

Edison's Black Maria

W. K. L. Dickson / Edison laboratory
West Orange, New Jersey, USA

Performers and camera operators worked within a compact studio to record short films and experiment with moving images. The building brought the stage, camera and available sunlight into a controlled relationship for film production.

The timber studio was covered in black tar paper and mounted on a circular track. Its roof opened and the building rotated to follow the sun, turning the architecture itself into a piece of filming equipment when early cameras needed strong natural light.
